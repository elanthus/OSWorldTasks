### C1 — Task and study scope

> We evaluated history and stateless policies for `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` on
> 192 paired cases from six families of a versioned synthetic GUI workload. The
> action-extraction successor reused inspected tasks and is a post-hoc parser-sensitivity study.

Basis: the frozen successor plan, full projection and structured sensitivity analysis.

### C2 — Primary observed comparison

> In that post-hoc study, history succeeded on 132/192 primary cases and stateless
> on 20/192. The paired difference was 58.33 percentage points, with a 95%
> family-stratified logical-cluster bootstrap interval of 50.80–65.80 points.
> This estimate describes the inspected workload; it does not establish independent
> confirmation or a causal effect of the extraction adapter.

Basis: `sensitivity.paired_absolute_difference` and `success_rates` in the structured
analysis. The exploratory Wilson intervals are not substituted for this paired interval.

### C3 — Complete outcome retention

> All 432 full-campaign assignments were retained: 172 successes, 257 step-limit
> terminations and three invalid outputs. The 48 reliability assignments and four
> separate pilot assignments were excluded from the 192 primary pairs.

Basis: full projection `counts`/`completed`, analysis reliability section and pilot projection.

### C4 — Accounting with unresolved usage

> The full campaign journal recorded 11,334 model-attempt reservations, 11,334
> provider-wire request reservations, 11,326 environment actions and zero provider
> control requests. Five confirmed-stopped post-send timeouts retain unknown
> provider completion and usage. Each received one permitted same-request retry;
> both reservations remain counted. Reported usage is incomplete for those five attempts.

Basis: full projection `phase_usage`, `unresolved_timeouts` and structured analysis accounting.
This is journal accounting, not a provider-side usage or billing audit.

### C5 — No-call integration observations

> On one Darwin arm64 host, the no-call refresh recorded 18 passing browser tests,
> five passing CLI/kernel sandbox tests and one passing OSWorld reconnect test.
> The CLI checks used placeholder credentials and refused provider egress. These
> observations cover the tested synthetic workload and host, not general desktop
> correctness or cross-platform isolation.

Basis: raw records 04–06 and their hash-bound runtime provenance in the fresh supplement.

### C6 — Evidence recovery

> Pilot and full evidence were each restored from both retained local copies.

> environment action replay were not demonstrated by this drill.

Basis: the fresh restore receipt. The separate audit views supply only the published,
hash-bound final retention receipt, after proving all other summary fields identical.

### C7 — Revision-bound engineering checks

> At PR #265 head `37d85e3912b54604025f15a964e055cd66e0d619`, hosted CI recorded
> 3,298 passing unit tests, six skips and 83.66% coverage of `pixelgym`. The six

Basis: the copied CI observation and the fresh skip investigation. Coverage applies
to `pixelgym`, not the new restore script or every file in the repository.

## Wording outside the supported scope

Do not approve wording that turns these observations into any of the following:

- Independent confirmation of a general advantage for history or any model ranking.
- A measured causal improvement from the extraction adapter, or a claim that prose
  extraction explains every remaining failure.
- Broad OSWorld task performance, cross-platform bitwise determinism, production
  readiness, universal sandbox safety, or kernel-enforced provider-hostname filtering.
- Complete provider usage/billing settlement, independent off-host disaster recovery,
  public raw-journal replay, or a claim that every test ran without skips.
- A declared D5.10 verdict, an approval of public wording, or authorization for more model calls.
