# Note, 2026-11-13 (UTC): what is wrong with this experiment

This folder is kept unchanged. Its result (0 of 400 closed-loop successes, and a click-target
accuracy of 13.3% in seven of eight runs, per `results.json`) reflects how the experiment was built.
It is not a finding about the task. The successor is
[`artifacts/bc-experiment-v2/`](../bc-experiment-v2/), produced by
`scripts/run_bc_experiment_v2.py`.

1. **Click head.** In `scripts/run_bc_experiment.py`, the click logits come from a 1x1
   convolution on the stem's 48x64 feature map. Its receptive field is 17 pixels of the 384x512
   observation, so it cannot see which fields are filled or where the form is. It was trained on
   a single-cell label. Version 2 gives the click branch coordinate channels and the trunk's global
   vector, and trains on every cell that lands on the oracle's control.
2. **Hidden state.** The frames were recorded under input contract v2. Under v2, a trailing space
   and the oracle's Enter on the country select left the frame unchanged, so some frames came right
   before two different oracle actions. That limited any single-frame policy. Input contract v3
   (#279) draws a caret, and the oracle no longer presses Enter on the select;
   `tests/unit/test_frame_determines_oracle_action.py` checks that no frame comes right before two
   different actions.
3. **Limits section.** `REPORT.md`'s Limits section is fixed prose in
   `scripts/generate_bc_report.py`. It attributes the failure to compounding errors and the lack of
   DAgger, which the stored results do not test.
4. **No baselines.** No baseline was reported beside any metric. Version 2 reports always-KEY,
   constant click, card-blind keys, a lookup table, and the oracle.
5. **Names.** The generator has 48 company names. The training pool (seeds 0–255) contains every
   company name used by the test seeds (10000–10049). Version 2 measures this overlap and adds a
   split that holds 12 names out of training.
