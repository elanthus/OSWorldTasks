## Why there is a chance level

Each task has two deferred choices with 3 options. The reference that decides each one appeared only on an earlier screen; the decision screen says "The source <request|verification> reference is no longer visible." A stateless policy sees only the instruction and the current screenshot, and both choices must be right for the submission to succeed.

- Uniform guess, otherwise perfect: probability 1/9, 21.3 of 192 episodes expected.
- Best fixed on-screen positions (1, 2), otherwise perfect: 26 of 192.

## Scope

Reference levels assume a policy that is perfect everywhere except the two consumer choices. They are not proven upper bounds for every possible stateless policy. The three campaigns ran on the in-process PIL backend (pixelgym/grounding/v5/backend.py and its memory-focus subclass), not a browser or OSWorld.
